Privacy

MOOP Map collects photographs of litter, pinned to where they were taken, so that community groups can show what a place looked like before and after a cleanup. Anyone can submit one. There is no account and no sign-in.

This page says exactly what is collected and where it ends up. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What you send

When you submit a report, these go with it:

  • The photograph, exactly as your camera took it.
  • Where your device says you are — latitude, longitude and the accuracy your phone reported. A location is required, because a photo that cannot be placed cannot go on a map.
  • The organization you picked from the list.
  • The time the report arrived.
  • Your browser's user agent — the line every website receives, naming your browser and operating system version. It is kept because browsers differ in whether they will share a location at all, and when reports fail it is often the only clue why.

Nothing identifies you. There is no name, no email address, no account, no advertising identifier, and nothing that follows you between visits.

Where it goes

Submissions arrive in a Google Drive folder and a Google Sheet belonging to the person who runs this map. A human reviews every batch before anything is published.

Accepted photographs are then uploaded to Mapillary, which is where the dots on this map come from. Once there they are public and permanent. Mapillary automatically blurs faces and number plates, but treat anything you photograph as published: do not send pictures of people, of the inside of anyone's home or vehicle, or of anything you would not want on a public map with its location attached.

What stays in your browser

A few small settings are kept on your own device so the site behaves sensibly on your next visit. They never leave your browser and are not readable by us:

  • the organization you last reported for
  • whether the map's panel was open or collapsed
  • which basemap you chose
  • which organization's area the map last showed you
  • a short-lived copy of the photo list, so revisiting the map is quick

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of them. The site sets no cookies.

What this site does not do
  • No analytics, of any kind.
  • No advertising, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • No tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party trackers.
  • No background location. Your position is read when you open the report form and while it is in front of you, never otherwise.
Other services this site loads

Like any website, the services whose content it loads can see your IP address when it does so. This site loads from:

  • OpenStreetMap and Esri — map tiles
  • Mapillary — the photographs and their locations
  • unpkg — the Leaflet mapping library
  • Google Apps Script — when the report page opens, to check the form is working, and again when you submit a report
Taking a photo down

Email bwb-south-bay@googlegroups.com and say roughly where and when the photo was taken. If it has not been published yet it is deleted. If it is already on Mapillary we will ask them to remove it there as well, and track that request until it is done.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it. The whole site is open source, so the history of this page is public at github.com/ccarfi/moopmapproto.
